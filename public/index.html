Why this works

Where you're insured matters as much as the accident.

Carriers price the same driver, the same crash, wildly differently — because they don't expect you to leave. That gap only survives on inertia. Quotely exists to close it.

  • The inertia tax. A $2,400 policy quietly becomes $2,760 at renewal — drivers pay the extra $360/yr because comparing takes too long.
  • It compounds nationally. 6.14 million police-reported crashes happen every year in the U.S. — each one triggers this same pricing gap for someone.
